Group Project · High School Graduation · 2024

Health and Happiness

A collaborative Arabic-language web project built during my final year at secondary school in 2024, developed as a graduation project alongside a team of four classmates.

The website aims to build a healthier community by helping users find trusted health food stores, dietary schedules, and personalized recommendations — all based on their specific health condition.
